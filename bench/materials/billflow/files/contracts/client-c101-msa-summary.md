# Master services agreement: summary for the billing team

Client: Quillfeather Provisions Co. (client id c-101)
Our entity: Kestrel Works Ltd.
Status: signed; renews 2027-03-01 unless either side gives 90 days' notice.
Confidential: commercial terms. Do not share outside Kestrel Works.

## Scope

Kestrel Works provides the metered invoicing platform, the nightly bill run, statement delivery
by e-mail and the monthly accounting export. Professional services outside this scope are
quoted separately and need a signed change order.

## Commercial terms

The client is billed monthly in arrears for metered usage. Rates follow the standard volume
tiers plus a negotiated extra discount that is recorded in the rates register. The extra
discount is reviewed at renewal and may be withdrawn if the client's monthly volume falls below
the second tier for three consecutive months.

Statements are issued by the third working day of the month. Disputed items must be reported
within fourteen days of the statement date; undisputed items stay payable on the due date.

Payment terms: net 45 days from the statement date. Late payments accrue interest at the
daily rate agreed in schedule 2, capped at fifteen percent of the invoiced amount. Interest
accrues from the day after the due date and is invoiced with the next statement.

All amounts are in TWD. Taxes are added at the statutory rate and shown as a separate line.

## Service levels

- Statement delivery: by 09:00 on the statement day, 99% of months.
- Accounting export: available by 12:00 on the fifth working day.
- Support response: one working day for billing questions, four hours for outages of the
  bill run affecting more than one statement.

Service credits are limited to ten percent of the monthly invoice and are applied to the next
statement. Credits are the sole remedy for missed service levels.

## Data protection

Kestrel Works processes the client's contact data only to deliver statements and reminders.
Personal data is kept for seven years for accounting reasons and then deleted. Sub-processors
are the mail delivery provider and the database host; the client is notified of changes thirty
days in advance.

## Termination

Either side may terminate for material breach after thirty days' written notice if the breach
is not cured. On termination the client receives a final statement and a full export of its
invoice history within ten working days.

## Liability

Liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim. Neither side is
liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## Notes for the billing team

The contract and the client register are maintained separately, so the terms in the register
can differ from the signed terms. When they differ, the signed contract wins; tell the account
manager so the register is corrected.
